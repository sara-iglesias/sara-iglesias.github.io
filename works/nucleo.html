Details

Núcleo is a chair constructed from an interlocking system of MDF ribs that form the internal skeleton of each piece. This framework reveals a structural logic in which every component gives stability and meaning to the whole.

Structure

The parts are connected through a central core: the seat, the only soft module, padded and upholstered, which balances the rigid structures. Each module can exist independently, but when assembled, the parts reveal a new totality: the whole is greater than the sum of its parts.

Exhibition

Núcleo took part in the exhibition De la silla al infinito (From the Chair to Infinity) at Museo Moderno, Buenos Aires. Alongside it, a series of public workshops were held at the museum, inviting participants to build and engage with the museum through a playful, design-driven lens.
