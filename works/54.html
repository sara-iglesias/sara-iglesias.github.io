The project

+54 is a visual identity system for an exhibition of Argentine design objects. Concept, curation, and visual language come together to build an identity rooted in local culture, with a pop sensibility.

Collection

The exhibition gathers 15 objects that take everyday elements of Argentine life — from home appliances to signage — and reinterpret them through a twist that combines humor, memory, and critique. The collection is organized into two settings: la casa and la calle, the domestic and the urban.

The name

+54 is Argentina's international dialing code, a minimal sign that signals belonging. Like the prefix itself, the exhibition operates through recognition: everyday objects that only make full sense if you're from here.

Visual System

The identity is built on a saturated, high-contrast palette: magenta, lime yellow and pink. It evokes a pop sensibility.

The exhibition

The +54 exhibition takes the form of a house built from four modular cubes that define the spaces: living room, kitchen, patio, bedroom. This structure travels through iconic locations across Buenos Aires, inserting itself into the same everyday context from which the objects emerged. Inside, the pieces are displayed with the same irony with which they were conceived.
