Details

From the classic processes of metal embossing, the forja® project develops a lighting system manufactured exclusively through this production method. The collection is designed using existing molds to optimize material use and production efficiency.

Production

Operating in small-scale batches, forja® integrates into the idle times of larger industrial workflows. By relying on national manufacturing processes and locally sourced metals, it reduces logistics costs while working with machinery dedicated to a single production technique.

Assembly

Stainless steel is used for 100% of the lamps, selected for its malleability, durability, and resilient finish. All models are produced from three pre-existing matrices, chosen according to their dimensions and productive feasibility. Each piece incorporates a 10mm central slot that allows for a welded-free interlocking assembly.
