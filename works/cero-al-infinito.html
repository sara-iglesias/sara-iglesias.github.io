The project

Cero al Infinito (Zero to Infinity) is an integrated proposal for a cultural circuit in Parque Los Andes (Chacarita, Buenos Aires), dissolving the physical and symbolic boundaries between Chacarita Cemetery and the surrounding community.

The problem

The project addresses the physical and symbolic neglect of both the Sixth Pantheon of Chacarita Cemetery and its architect, Ítala Fluvia Villa, restoring visibility to a significant yet overlooked architectural and cultural legacy.

Where

Located in one of Buenos Aires' fastest-growing cultural districts, the proposal responds to Chacarita's transformation into a hub for gastronomy, audiovisual production, tourism, and emerging creative practices.

Where

Parque Los Andes remains an underused public space. The project reimagines it as a connector, channeling the neighborhood's cultural activity toward the cemetery through a continuous public route.

The solution

Visibility creates value. An elevated pathway acts as both a public route and a lookout over the cemetery, inviting visitors to engage with a place that has long remained disconnected from everyday urban life.

The intervention is reinforced by a museum and a public fair, activating the surroundings while strengthening the relationship between the cemetery and the city.

The museum

The Pioneers Museum celebrates pioneers across generations, from historical figures such as Ítala Fluvia Villa to contemporary innovators, creating a dialogue between past and present while preserving their legacy.

Concept

Cero al Infinito reflects the project's central idea: zero represents the pioneers and the origin, while infinity symbolizes the continuity of their legacy.

The circle becomes the project's defining symbol, expressing continuity, permanence, and living memory.
